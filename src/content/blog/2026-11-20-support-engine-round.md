Every change to the support engine is measured on the same 13 scenes before it goes in: solid and hollow miniatures, plates of several models, a large hollow whale, a castle, a worm. For each scene the scorecard records how many islands the review still finds, how many supports stand on the model, how many could not be placed, braces, resin and time ([how the scorecard works](/blog/how-supports-are-placed/)).

## What changed

- **Nothing rests on the model for no reason.** A point with model 2 layers below it needs no support: the layer under it holds it. The engine now checks this before placing, telling inside from outside by counting crossings, not by parity.
- **Supports are not lost when a trunk goes.** When the final check removes a trunk that ran into the model, the supports that hung from it were removed with it. They are now placed again from their own starting point, and checked against every model on the plate.
- **A raft per model.** Each model can have its own raft settings: type, thickness, or none.
- **Solid rafts with more options.** A solid raft can now have pads under the trunks instead of one slab, holes, and peel tabs. Peel tabs go at the raft's sharpest corners, the places where peeling starts, up to 4, 15 mm apart.
- **Supports only from the plate.** A preset option for models that should never carry a support on their own surface.
- **Drains stay clear.** No tip goes inside the channel of a drain. A raft that covers a drain hole is drawn anyway, with a warning.
- **Groups.** Models grouped together are placed, supported and given one raft as one unit.
- **Fewer, better placed island tips.** Island contours now get points along the contour instead of a grid over its cells: 6 % fewer tips, the same islands covered.

From the start of the round to its end, on the 13 scenes:

| | Start | End |
|---|---|---|
| Islands left in the review | 132 | 131 |
| Island supports standing on the model | 637 | 48 |
| Points with no tip near them | 282 | 116 |
| Supports that could not be placed | 3,249 | 2,045 |
| Supports going through a model | 0 | 0 |
| Braces | 29,550 | 32,533 |
| Support resin | | +1.6 % |
| Time to generate | | −7 % |

The second half of the round brought tips from 35,571 to 33,282 and support resin down 2.5 %, with the same 131 islands.

## A tip with no room

Some contacts sit where no straight tip fits: under a ledge, between two folds. Those used to be dropped. Now, and only for those, the tip may come in at 45°. The review went from 131 islands to 122, and supports whose foot stands on the model from 6,168 to 5,588. It costs 6 % more support resin.

## Measured and left out

- **Merging tips closer than 0.8 mm.** It cut the pairs of tips closer than 0.8 mm from 3,531 to 969, but raised the islands in the review from 132 to 207. Left out.
- **Refusing tips by the angle between tip and face.** In the 13 scenes, no tip was ever refused by it. Nothing to change.

## A hair under the plate

The plate showed striped under a plate of supports. A brace that starts on the plate and leans left a ring 0.0075 mm under it, and that showed. Everything below the plate is now set on it.
