Breadslicer 0.5.0-beta.1 is out: the first open beta of Breadslicer, a native resin (MSLA) slicer for Windows, free software under the AGPL-3.0. Everything the posts of the last week described as "in the next build" is in it.

It now comes with an **installer**, [`Breadslicer-0.5.0-beta.1-windows-x64-setup.exe`](https://github.com/breadslicer-app/breadslicer/releases/download/v0.5.0-beta.1/Breadslicer-0.5.0-beta.1-windows-x64-setup.exe) (42.5 MB). It needs no administrator rights. Once it is installed, `.bread` files open with Breadslicer and show their thumbnail in Explorer, and Breadslicer appears in "Open with" for STL, 3MF and OBJ. If you prefer not to install anything, the [portable zip](https://github.com/breadslicer-app/breadslicer/releases/download/v0.5.0-beta.1/Breadslicer-0.5.0-beta.1-windows-x64.zip) (57.0 MB) is still there: unzip it anywhere and run `breadslicer.exe`. Both are on the [release page on GitHub](https://github.com/breadslicer-app/breadslicer/releases/tag/v0.5.0-beta.1).

## New since 0.4.0-alpha.1

- **Supports.** Pick any part of a support and move it with the gizmo: trunks, elbows, knots, tips. There are Line and Area tools, supports on a face's edge or in a circle, and mirrored supports, and **H** hides the supports you picked. Supports keep clear of the models next to them, also after you move things. No tip is thinner than three pixels of your printer's screen. More in [Supports by hand](/blog/supports-by-hand/) and [A round on the support engine](/blog/support-engine-round/).
- **Forces.** Every layer has a forces card. The supports are painted by how hard their tips pull when a layer peels. A full structural check treats the supports as a beam frame, and a button reinforces the weak ones. See [How hard each layer pulls](/blog/peel-force/).
- **Arrange and Fill bed.** New packing engines fit more copies on a plate and use fewer plates for large batches, with up to six different models mixed. Mark the dead zones of your printer's screen, and Arrange keeps away from them. See [Two more minis per plate](/blog/arrange-denser/).
- **Prepare.** Lean a model back by an exact angle in one click. Each model takes the support preset of its height, and a model can be scaled to fit. Cut along a stroke or a lasso you draw, with pins and magnet holes in the cuts, and use Orient (Full), which avoids suction cups. See [Cut along a line you draw](/blog/cutting-and-placing/).
- **Files.** Open scenes saved by another resin slicer, with their supports and hollows. Reload a model from disk, also by itself when its file changes. Imported files keep their part names, exports take a file name template, and `.bread` files carry their creator's credit. See [Supports saved as data](/blog/supports-as-data/).
- **Printers and resins.** There are now 171 printers, each with [its own page](/printers/). ELEGOO's own resin settings cover 25 printers, the Mars 5 Ultra and Saturn 4 Ultra included. Breadslicer now writes Prusa SL1 and SL1S files (`.sl1`/`.sl1s`), ships Phrozen's resin tables, and slices adaptive layers into CTB v3 and newer. See [178 printers with a face](/blog/printers-and-resins-two/); since then the list has dropped the entries that were screen upgrade kits, not printers.
- **Network printers.** Send prints to ELEGOO printers over Wi-Fi (SDCP), watch their cameras, and keep your printers with their own names, profiles and notes. See [Sending a print over Wi-Fi](/blog/wifi-printing/).
- **Review and repair.** Models are repaired on import, the review card floats over the view, and drains that miss the cavity show in red. Many fixes came from testers' reports. See [Around the view](/blog/around-the-view/) and [Drains in a model that is already hollow](/blog/drains-in-a-finished-hollow/).
- **New version check.** It is off unless you turn it on in Preferences > Privacy. When it is on, the app asks GitHub for the list of releases when it opens, and that request is all that leaves your computer.

## Before you print

The printer tested on real prints is the **Anycubic Photon Mono M7 Pro**. On any other printer, check the first print closely: the model in the right place on the plate, text not mirrored, and the right exposure.

The installer and the exe are not signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Reporting problems

**Help > Export Diagnostics...** saves a zip that helps us understand a problem, without your folder paths or user name. Nothing is ever sent on its own. Report on the [issues page](https://github.com/breadslicer-app/breadslicer/issues) or on [Discord](/community/).
