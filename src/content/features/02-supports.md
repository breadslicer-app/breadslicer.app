**Generate** makes trunks, branches, tips, bracing and a raft for every selected model, each in its own set, in one undo step. Move a model or turn it on the plate and its supports come along; tilt or scale it and they are made again for the new pose.

By hand, **A** shows a green support under the cursor, already routed to the plate, and says why when one cannot attach. It hooks onto nearby trunks, **Shift+click** forces one, and **1** to **4** pick the tip size. **M** drags a tip across the surface. **X** removes, by click, box or brush (**B**), and only touches supports you can actually see.
