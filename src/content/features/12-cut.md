Cut a model with a flat plane, across the whole model or only inside an outline you shape by its corners, with handles in the scene. Click the parts of the section you want cut. The cut stays closed and exact, and a fallback catches what it cannot handle; see [Cutting a model with a plane](/blog/plane-cut/).

Cut along a line you draw across the model or around a region with a lasso, or let Breadslicer suggest a cut from the model's creases. A stroke or lasso stays on the model as a cutting surface you can move and tilt from any side before it cuts, and the part that runs through the model shows in green. Cuts can add pins or magnet slots, and the pieces of a supported model get their own supports.
