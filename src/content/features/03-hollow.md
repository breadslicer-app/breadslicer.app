The translucent cavity follows the wall setting as you change it, in ±1 and ±0.1 mm steps, with a recommended thickness one click away. The wall is measured as the exact distance to the model's surface, and the preview *is* the result: hollowing places exactly the cavity on screen, so a drain you aim at the cavity reaches it.

Read how it works in the report [Exact hollowing: the preview is the result](/reports/exact-hollowing/).
